# Phase 10 — Pharmacy safety: dispensing errors, near misses, product concerns

> **Not clinically validated. Not production-ready.** These are **internal pharmacy records** for a pharmacy's own review and learning. They are not regulatory reports, not a nationally mandated classification, and not proof that anything is wrong with a product. Nothing here is sent to a manufacturer, regulator or insurer. Persian wording is **awaiting clinical review**. MedSmarter is a **facilitator** of internal records; it is not a manufacturer, importer, national reporting authority or legally responsible reporting party.

Evidence labels (as in Phase 9): **[SLIDE]** lecture slide · **[SUPP]** named supplementary source · **[DESIGN]** design proposal · **[OPEN]** needs authoritative confirmation · **[BLOCKED]** needs clinical/regulatory/legal/privacy/expert approval.
